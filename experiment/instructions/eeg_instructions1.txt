Welcome to our EEG experiment and thank you for your participation!


In this experiment you will read texts on the screen. All texts will be followed by a couple of multiple-choice questions that you should answer.


During this time we will measure your brain waves. To do so, we have placed an EEG cap with numerous integrated electrodes on your head. These electrodes have been filled with a conductive gel to reduce the electrical resistance between the electrodes and your scalp. The whole procedure is non-invasive and does not pose any risks to you.


Try to maintain a comfortable and upright position during the experiment and avoid excessive movements.
